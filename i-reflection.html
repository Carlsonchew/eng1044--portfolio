Alex Yeo Wei Onn Completed ▶
Self & Peer-Review Reflection390 words

For this project, conducting the research wasn't too difficult for me, as I had done a similar task in the Intensive English Program (IEP) in 2023. It mainly involved recalling how to conduct research effectively. However, this time was different because it was my first time completing a research project as a team.

We encountered some challenges along the way. For example, we needed to find literature from 2021 to 2026, much of which focused on the COVID-19 pandemic, making it quite difficult to find references relevant to our specific situation. I also improved my citation formatting: previously, I relied on directly copying APA formatting from Google Scholar or manually formatting it in Word, but this time I used professional citation websites like MyBib, which was not only much more convenient but also reduced formatting errors.

Through this project, I gained several important personal insights about writing and citations. First, I realized the importance of carefully choosing words and emphasis; for example, I focused on "interests" while a teammate focused on "motivations," which made it easy for me to inadvertently overlap with his points in my writing. Going forward, I must be careful to avoid overlapping with his points to allow more room to develop my own sections. Furthermore, in researching references, I learned to carefully verify that the arguments in the literature are completely consistent with my specific viewpoints.

Working with the team made me realize the unique and important contributions each member made to the project. Ker Zhen provided crucial technical and constructive support, helping to build the portfolio website. I personally gained some writing inspiration from his paragraph formatting. Kyal made significant contributions to team planning and quality control; he helped team members create mind maps, brainstorm initial topics, and meticulously checked various details in the work, such as word count. My advice for the future is that we should actively promote teamwork. In future team tasks, we should frequently hold open discussions to ensure smooth communication and efficient work.

In conclusion, this research experience helped me identify areas for improvement. Going forward, I will prioritize more in-depth and frequent team discussions to ensure smooth communication at each stage. I will also carefully coordinate research points and references, meticulously check word counts and other relevant regulations, and make full use of reliable tools such as MyBib.

This research project has helped me understand more about the impact of gamification on students. As I am a student myself, this topic was highly relevant to myself, and has encouraged me to look into the gamified online platforms to make revision and studying more excitingly.

It takes effort to keep up, to focus and comprehend the long texts and summarizing the main points. Sometimes I read the same paper several times before I got the gist of it. The moment of understanding was very rewarding, especially after I had comprehend the main points of the research papers. It was also really a great accomplishment when the paper was completed as it was tough and exhausting but a great learning experience. Another challenge I face mainly is that I am quite inexperienced in writing academically and made some mistakes like APA referencing format. In the process, I consistently refer to academic writing format online, together with resource provided on elearn, and eventually corrected the mistakes I made and grew more comfortable with the process of academic writing and more comfortable with reading and interpreting research papers, and I believe it improves my ability to deal with difficulties that I initially thought I could not. I also worked on creating the portfolio website. So overall, the most important lesson I learnt is that it is very important to take initiative in learning, and find solutions even if it is difficult and learn in the process.

Alex's contributions to teamwork were in the introduction and spark interest section, and he created and checked the references. Having the opportunity to work with him has taught me the importance of making sure that everyone knows what they have to do when breaking up research tasks. I am sure, In the future, I will be more experienced and take more initiative to use these skills when I write future academic work as a student.

Kyal Sinn Ye Htut Completed ▶
Self & Peer-Review Reflection523 words

Mostly, I've learned some basic concepts of gamification and how it can be implemented for educational purposes. I've tested a few features of gamification learning on myself, and it has helped me study better. Similarly to the result of the research, gamification actually boosts my self-regulation and motivation when it comes to learning. Therefore, I would say I mostly gained a positive learning experience from this research.

There was one incident that made me stay up all night due to AI detection. I had written the paragraphs all by myself by looking at proper grammar structure, but it showed above 60% AI. However, I managed to resolve that issue and got under 20% thankfully.

With the increasing popularity of AI, I admit that I have been relying on it more and more for my studies. So, when I had to write the essay without it, I had to relearn basic grammar, and that became a wake-up call for me. Since then, I've been trying to use AI less and less, and I have managed to practise my writing and improve significantly. I've learned to enjoy writing personal journals as well.

My task was mostly editing and integrating the whole essay and submitting it. Each of us was responsible for one part of the essay. I gathered all those parts, reformatted and edited them, and made sure nothing was missing.

Alex did most of the citation formatting and gathering of citations. He also submitted on Padlet for us every week. Ker Zhen worked on the technical side of this project. He made the portfolio template and invited us to edit it. I would say Ker Zhen is the builder, Alex is the managing director, and I am the executive officer in this project.

I have learned mostly about academic sentence structure and how to read research articles effectively. I also think I have broadened my range of vocabulary, and truth be told, it has made me feel more confident whenever I try to use new words in my daily life.

However, if I had to do things differently, I would organise my notes from reading articles better. I would have written notes with the page number and article names, including the in-text citations. Then, I would make separate files for each note according to the articles and summarise them in my own words first before jumping straight into writing the essay. I did things very messily, so I had a hard time organising my notes and remembering which article they came from. However, I think it is a good thing now that I have learned how to do it properly.

In the future, when I participate in or assist with more research projects, these new skills will be very useful. Since I am a research enthusiast, I can see myself doing more of these tasks in the future. Hopefully, I can work in the new Web3 industry. There is so much research to do for this emerging technology. I am glad that I have gained these skills, and I can now use them properly to contribute to the greater good of the research field.
