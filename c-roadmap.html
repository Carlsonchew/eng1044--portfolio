Weekly Roadmap — Week 1 to 13

Our group's planned milestones mapped to the assignment's 11-step process.

Week 1–2 · Step 1

Pre-research Completed

Conduct preliminary library/online research to find a suitable ICT topic.
Our plan: We discussed as a group and decided on our topic, which centers on gamification in online learning platforms.

Week 1–2 · Step 2

Establishing Portfolio Platform Completed

Decide on a suitable online platform and plan how to share the project output publicly.
Our plan: We decided to use GitHub Pages to build and host our e-portfolio website.

Week 1–3 · Step 3

Topic Approval Completed

Narrow down a research topic and get instructor approval; consult on platform and sharing.
Our plan: We discussed our topic with Miss Siti and finalized it as "The Positive Effects of Gamification on Students' Online Learning Platforms."

Week 1–3 · Step 4

Building Portfolio Foundation Completed

Create table of contents, label all sections (a–i), complete items a, b, c. Submit link to instructor.
Our plan: We set up our e-portfolio structure, built the table of contents, and completed the cover page and checklist (items a, b, c), then submitted the link to our instructor.

Week 2–4 · Step 5

Gathering Sources & Constructing Outline Completed

Detailed academic research; build outline with min. 2–3 main ideas.
Our plan: We gathered and searched for suitable research papers and began building our main ideas.

Week 5 · Step 6

Outline Consultation Completed

Consult instructor on outline; revise and finalize; add to portfolio as item (d).
Our plan: We built and finalized our outline and added it to the e-portfolio.

Week 5–8 · Step 7

Selecting Relevant Literature Completed

In-depth research; select sources to support the paper; add to portfolio as item (g).
Our plan: We gathered more sources and selected the ones that best support our paper, then added them to the e-portfolio as item (g).

Week 6–11 · Step 8

Writing Assignments & Drafts Completed

Draft the argumentative essay per outline; cite all sources in APA 7th edition; include CW2 + CW3 drafts as items (e), (f), (h).
Our plan: We began working on the drafts of our research paper and completed the final paper.

Week 12–13 · Step 9

Share Completed

Share the project with a target audience beyond classmates; record evidence as item (h).
Our plan: We shared our project with other students.

Week 13 · Step 10

Reflection Completed

Each member writes a max-500-word self & peer reflection; compiled as item (i).
Our plan: Each member wrote and completed their individual self and peer reflection.

Week 13 · Step 11

Submission Completed

Allow instructor to duplicate/access the final portfolio (adjust sharing settings).
Our plan: We submitted the final paper.
